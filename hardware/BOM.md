# Bill of Materials

- Arduino Uno
- HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
- SG90/MG90S (or similar) Servo Motor
- 16x2 I2C LCD (commonly 0x27 address)
- 2 × LEDs (Green, Red)
- 2 × 220Ω resistors for LEDs
- Buzzer (active recommended)
- Momentary push button switch
- Jumper wires, breadboard or PCB
- 5V power source capable of driving servo + peripherals
